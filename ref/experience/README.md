# Lived experience

This folder is for lived experience: one person's own account of what happened, what helped and what the system did not see. In Norwegian this is called *erfaringskunnskap*, and health services recognise it as knowledge of its own kind, next to research and professional expertise.

Lived experience is not evidence, and it is never presented as evidence. It shows what one life looked like from the inside. That is often exactly what the economic story leaves out.

## Rules

1. **Your own experience only.** An account is written by the person it is about, or with their explicit consent. No one writes another person's story.
2. **Clearly marked.** Every account starts with the same line: *This is one person's lived experience. It is not evidence and not advice.*
3. **No advice.** An account says what happened and what helped this person. It does not tell anyone else what to do.
4. **No names of health workers or other private people.** Institutions may be named when it matters. Individuals are not.
5. **You choose the level of detail.** Share only what you are comfortable with. Data such as timelines, tests or measurements are welcome, at the level you choose.
6. **Public and permanent.** This repository is public, and its history cannot be changed afterwards. Anything published here can be copied and stays in the history, even if the file is later removed. Think it through before you publish.
7. **Every account ends with the same notice:** *Do not change your own treatment based on this. Talk to a doctor or someone you trust.*

## How to add an account

Copy [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) to a new file, for example `2026-firstname.md`, and fill it in. Accounts are published only after the person they are about has read the final version and said yes.
