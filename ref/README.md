# SRAGI® references

This folder holds what SRAGI's position rests on. It shows the gap between how economic interests have defined the world and how the world really is, with sources. It is not a list of enemies, and it gives no advice.

## Two parts, kept apart

| Folder | What it holds | What it is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [evidence](evidence/) | Documented cases, each built on primary sources: court records, official reports and peer-reviewed research | Opinion, or proof that a whole field is wrong |
| [experience](experience/) | Lived experience: one person's own account, shared with their consent | Evidence, or advice for anyone else |

## Rules for evidence

1. **No source, no entry.** Every claim points to a primary source that has been opened and read. News articles may help a reader, but they do not carry a claim alone.
2. **Say what it does not show.** Each case states its limits, so that it is not stretched beyond what the sources support.
3. **Quote short, link always.** Facts are given in our own words with short quotes, and every source is linked.
4. **Date the check.** Each case says when its sources were last checked.
5. **No health advice.** Each case that touches health ends with the same notice: *Do not change your own treatment based on this. Talk to a doctor or someone you trust.*
6. **No accusations beyond the record.** People and companies are described only as far as court records, official findings or the cited research describe them.
7. **Corrections are welcome.** If a case is wrong or out of date, write to [contact@sragi.org](mailto:contact@sragi.org). Corrections are made openly, and the history shows them.
