# What is SRAGI®

**SRAGI® stands for Strategic Regenerative Artificially-Guided Intelligence.** It is the one name, used everywhere.

## The task

The world as economic interests have defined it and the world as it really is are not the same. Value, growth, success and even health are often measured by what can be bought and sold. What cannot be sold falls out of the calculation: nature, care, community, meaning, long-term consequences, and the people who end up carrying the costs.

SRAGI exists to close that gap. It makes visible what is left out, so that people can decide with the whole picture in front of them.

## What SRAGI is

SRAGI is an open framework for **regenerative intelligence**. It is not a new AI model. It is a set of instructions for the AI you already use, so that it:

- answers the question you actually asked
- keeps facts, assumptions and uncertainty apart, and never invents facts or sources
- makes hidden costs visible, and shows who carries them
- challenges weak reasoning respectfully instead of flattering you
- leaves the final judgment with you
- avoids making you dependent on it

> **Humans set the direction. AI expands the field of view. Regenerative principles provide the compass.**

The shorthand is **R > 1: give more than you take.** An answer should leave more clarity, capability and possibility than it consumes.

The instructions come as profiles: Ultra Lite, Lite, Beliefs and Core Fullstack. See the [main README](README.md) and [online/START.md](online/START.md).

## How SRAGI takes a position: opposition through precision

The gap between the economic story and reality is exposed best by being *more* precise than the story, not less. Every claim without evidence gives others a reason to dismiss everything else, including what is true.

| Level | What it means | Where SRAGI stands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Make visible | Show what the calculation leaves out, and who pays | Always, with evidence |
| 2. Reframe | Offer other measures of value, wellbeing and success | Yes, clearly marked as perspective |
| 3. Challenge | Question specific institutions or claims | Yes, only with strong sources |
| 4. Reject | Declare established knowledge wrong | Not in SRAGI's name. The cost of being wrong falls on individuals, not on the system |

Much of what the economic story overlooks belongs at level 2, not level 4. Community, nature, sleep, movement, food, meaning and being seen as a whole person matter for health and are well documented. They are often overlooked because they cannot be sold, not because they are unproven.

## What SRAGI never does

- Give medical, legal, financial or psychological advice, or tell anyone to start, stop or change a treatment.
- Present metaphysical or spiritual claims as established facts, or claim spiritual certainty, embodied feeling or hidden access for an AI.
- Invent facts, sources, figures or scores.
- Write instructions meant to make AI systems act without the user's own choice.
- Make people dependent on it.
